ABOUT ME

My Journey

I recently made the jump from Occupational Health to Software Engineering after completing a web development bootcamp at Le Wagon.
During the program, I built a solid foundation in Ruby, SQL, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, and started applying those skills with Ruby on Rails to create real-world projects.

Right now, I'm focused on leveling up my front-end and back-end abilities, building things that solve actual problems, and improving both my own skills and those of the people around me.
I want to create tools that help friends, colleagues, and businesses in ways that make a real impact.

Outside of coding, I spend a lot of time with my family and love a good movie or series.
I'm a huge football fan—Chelsea all the way—and while I still enjoy playing, the years are catching up with me. These days, I'm more of a center-back, keeping the opposition onside whether I mean to or not.

Sundays are for church, which keeps me grounded and gives me space to reflect.
